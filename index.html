          How it works

          Private by design

          PagePress reads your files with the browser's own APIs and does the PDF work locally with pdf-lib and PDF.js. No upload queue, no stranger's server, nothing to leak.

          Real PDF editing

          Merging and extracting copy the actual pages out of your documents — text stays text and vectors stay sharp. Nothing is flattened into screenshots unless you ask for images.

          Honest about limits

          Password-protected PDFs cannot be opened — remove the password first. Very large files are limited by your device's memory, because everything happens on your machine.
